01

SHIFT CONTROL

Daily Procedures

9:00 a.m. opening checklist

  1. Start computers and log into Platinum; start the server first.
  2. Set adjustment and consultation rooms to the EHR screen; set the check-in computer to Arrivals.
  3. Run the X-ray computer test blank and turn on the SoftWave unit.
  4. Review and respond to Sked messages and voicemails.
  5. Enter new leads from approved lead sources and check the office inbox.
  6. Confirm any still-unconfirmed same-day NPM and ROF appointments; confirm the next business day's NPMs and ROFs.
  7. Check submitted intakes and upload them to the correct patient account.
  8. Verify pending insurance items, prioritizing upcoming appointments.
  9. Complete the room-readiness check below.
  10. Turn on the diffuser. Open curtains and unlock the front door at 10:00 a.m.

Room-readiness check

  • No trash in rooms
  • Tables cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed
  • Counters wiped
  • Bathroom clean and stocked
  • Face paper and paper towels stocked
  • Kids' area picked up
  • Spray bottles full; cloths replaced
  • SoftWave room stocked with gowns and cloths
  • Cups, napkins, tea, and water available

2:45 p.m. reset

  1. Reset the diffuser.
  2. Review texts and voicemails received during the break.
  3. Repeat the light-cleaning and room-readiness check.
  4. Open curtains and unlock the door at 3:00 p.m.

End-of-day checklist

  1. Confirm all next-day NPM and ROF appointments.
  2. Scan and upload all documents to the correct accounts.
  3. Schedule first-adjustment texts in Sked that need to send.
  4. Restock forms for the next day and finish next-day insurance verification.
  5. Take out trash; clean and reset rooms.
  6. Turn off SoftWave.
  7. Shut down computers and confirm the Platinum backup completes.
  8. If last out: check every room, lock the front glass and back office doors, and turn off lights and music.

Daily appointment and message review

  1. Open the Platinum calendar and review today first.
  2. Check every NPM and ROF appointment. If it is still unconfirmed, call before checking the patient in.
  3. Review the next business day and confirm all NPM and ROF appointments.
  4. Open Sked, voicemail, and the office inbox. Return messages and document each call with the date and your initials.
  5. Before leaving, make sure all first-adjustment messages are scheduled for the correct patient, date, and time.

When you have 10 minutes

Complete approved follow-up calls, research community events, work on approved health-talk outreach, straighten chairs and toys, clean counters/tables/windows/mirrors, refresh table paper, pick up debris, or shred only with prior approval and no patients present.

02

PATIENT JOURNEY

New Practice Member - Day 1

Day 1 procedure

  1. Prepare before arrival. Check online intake status and create/confirm the Platinum profile. Review name, address, phone, date of birth, referral source, insurance, and important F5 notes.
  2. Welcome by name. Introduce yourself. If forms are incomplete, provide the current intake. Request the driver's license and insurance card for copying/verification, then promptly return the originals.
  3. Confirm, update, then check in. Never check a patient in while the appointment is coded U or 2. Confirm it, change it to the correct confirmed code, update demographics and insurance, then mark the member arrived.
  4. Build the visit packet. Complete the two current red-pen forms: Consultation History/Day 1 and Exam Form/Range of Motion. Place them with the intake on the designated clipboard. Offer tea or water.
  5. Hand off personally. Give the clipboard to the patient coordinator and walk the member to an available consultation room.
  6. Clinical sequence. Consultation, doctor's examination, X-rays, and SoftWave mapping when applicable. Clinical decisions stay with the doctor.
  7. Close Day 1. Schedule the ROF, collect the approved Day 1 fee, provide the receipt, and explain that confirmation/reminder texts will follow.
  8. Finish documentation. Scan and upload intake, ID/insurance verification, Day 1 form, completed exam form, signed receipt when required, and any supporting records. Route paperwork per current manager instruction.
SUGGESTED WELCOME

"Welcome to Revive Chiropractic. My name is [name]. While you complete the application, I’ll copy your driver’s license and insurance information so we can verify it for you. Please let me know if you have any questions."

X-ray room safety handoff

Confirm the correct patient record on the X-ray computer. Instruct the patient to remove metal items and empty pockets as directed. Do not position, expose, interpret, or answer clinical questions beyond your training and authorization; notify the doctor when the patient is ready.

Day 1 boundaries: Do not discuss care-plan finances where other patients can hear. If asked about a same-day adjustment, explain that the doctor determines what is appropriate after the evaluation—do not promise or deny one.
03

PATIENT JOURNEY

Report of Findings (ROF)

Before and during

  1. Prepare. Confirm insurance verification and current benefit information. Have the Day 1 materials and manager-approved care plan ready.
  2. Receive. Check the patient in, offer tea/water, walk them to the consultation room, and give the prepared clipboard to the doctor.
  3. Process only the selected plan. Follow the current approved payment workflow for pay-in-full, monthly/recurring, or CareCredit. Never improvise pricing, discounts, or financing terms.
  4. Schedule care. Create the calendar, apply the correct appointment codes, issue the member card, and review how to swipe in.
  5. Orient to the adjustment room. Explain room numbers, where personal items go, computer check-in, and that table drops/noises are normal.
  6. Close the visit. After the adjustment, direct the patient to SoftWave or the front as the care plan requires and remind them of the next visit.
  7. Complete the account. Upload the signed care plan, receipt, recurring agreement, and CareCredit records if used; enter payment, plan notes, plan dates/cost/visits, services, coded visits, stop managers, and the first-adjustment message in Sked.
Payment control: Use only the option, amount, service code, recurrence date, and processor approved for that patient's signed plan. If any field conflicts, stop and ask management before charging or scheduling.

First-adjustment message timing

  1. Open Sked after the patient completes the first adjustment.
  2. If the adjustment was in the morning, schedule the message for that afternoon.
  3. If the adjustment was in the afternoon, schedule the message for the next morning.
  4. Before saving, check the patient name, phone number, template, send date, and send time.
04

PLATINUM WORKFLOW

Scheduling & Care Plans

Create the calendar after ROF

  1. Right-click in the appointment box and select Add.
  2. For a 36-visit plan, select Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday in the 3X column and enter the number of weeks listed on the patient’s signed plan.
  3. Enter the patient’s preferred time for each selected day. Review appointment history when trying to preserve their usual day and time.
  4. Exit to save, then open the Patient Calendar (pink box).
  5. Enter 1 at visit 2, E at visit 6, R at visits 12 and 24, X at visit 36, and P at visit 37.
  6. Print the calendar with 1 → C.

Set up the care plan

  1. Open the patient account and select Plan Start.
  2. Enter the actual care start date.
  3. Enter the patient portion from the signed care plan—not from memory.
  4. Enter the allowed number of visits.
  5. Select End Care Plan by Visits and Care Plan Option.
  6. If applicable, select Keep Billing Insurance.
  7. Exit to save; confirm the care-plan box turns blue.

Set up services

  1. Open the Service 1–6 link under the care plan.
  2. Set Service #1 to the correct adjustment service. For insurance, use INS-ADJ 3–4 and enter the verified copay.
  3. Use Service #3 for therapeutic exercises for ASH patients only.
  4. When insurance coverage or active care ends, switch the service to Wellness.

Add stop managers

  1. From F5 notes, open Stop Manager; use F4 to add entries.
  2. Add: 3 Block (if applicable), 7 Exercises, 12 remind first reassessment, 13 first reassessment, 23 remind second reassessment, 24 second reassessment, 35 remind re-X-ray, and 36 re-X-ray.
  3. Add a service-change reminder where insurance or active care ends.
  4. Add date-based reminders for payments, updated UHC forms, or other account needs.
  5. Review every entry against the signed plan before saving.

PATIENT FLOW

Current Patients

Standard visit

  1. Greet the patient and watch for alerts, stop managers, special codes, payment notes, and care-plan status.
  2. Have the patient swipe the member card at the front desk and again in the adjusting room.
  3. Complete NMR or other assigned exercises when applicable.
  4. Direct the patient to the next open, clean adjusting room.
  5. Complete checkout, payment, scheduling, and documentation required by the account.
EStart exercises

Teach assigned wobble-chair work, then direct to adjustment and assigned cervical traction. Use the approved explanation; do not prescribe or modify exercises.

RReassessment

Prepare for the scheduled re-exam, alert the patient coordinator/doctor, then continue flow as directed.

XRe-X-ray

Usually near the end of corrective care. After the assigned visit sequence, prepare the X-ray handoff and schedule the progressive ROF.

PProgressive ROF

Prepare before/after materials and alert the patient coordinator for the wellness-care discussion.

TSoftWave

Follow the patient's assigned SoftWave workflow and room protocol.

VPer visit

Collect only the approved per-visit rate shown in the current account note.

06

ASSIGNED CARE

Services & Therapies

NMR / wobble chair

  1. Confirm the exercise assignment/code before starting.
  2. Teach the approved wobble-chair sequence and supervise within your training.
  3. When finished, direct the patient to the next clean adjusting room.

Cervical traction

  1. Confirm the patient is assigned traction and verify the approved setup.
  2. Use only the position, repetitions, and timing directed for that patient.
  3. Stop and notify the clinician if the patient reports pain, dizziness, or concern.

SoftWave

  1. Turn on and stock the room during opening.
  2. Confirm the patient, treatment area, mapping/session type, and clinician instructions.
  3. Provide the approved aftercare sheet; do not add medical claims or advice.
  4. Clean, reset, and restock the room after use.

Decompression

  1. Check the patient’s prescribed level and timing before setup.
  2. Set the machine to the prescribed level and time.
  3. Document completion and tell the doctor about any discomfort or equipment concerns.

Other assigned therapies

Graston and any additional therapy are performed or directed only by appropriately trained clinical staff. Front-desk duties are to identify the appointment requirement, prepare the room/materials, maintain patient flow, and document or charge only the approved service.

Price reference

  • $59 new-patient special: consultation and X-rays
  • $29 referral new-patient special
  • $67 decompression
  • $47 for each therapy added to a new-patient special

Confirm the current price in Platinum before quoting or charging.

PATIENT COVERAGE

Insurance Verification

Verification workflow

  1. Prepare. Use the current verification form, member ID, date of birth, payer contact/portal, and authorized provider identifiers.
  2. Confirm eligibility. Record effective date, renewal date, chiropractic benefits, network status, copay or coinsurance, deductible and amount met, out-of-pocket maximum and amount met, visit limits/remaining visits, and authorization requirements.
  3. Use the approved channel. Follow current manager guidance for ASH, Availity, Optum/UHC, Medicare, or the number on the back of the card. Network participation changes; never promise coverage from memory.
  4. Document the source. Record representative name, call reference number, your name, date, portal result, and any pending request.
  5. Communicate carefully. Explain that verification is not a guarantee of payment and escalate unclear or conflicting benefits before quoting an amount.

COMMUNICATION

Phone & Follow-up

STANDARD GREETING

"Thank you for calling Revive Chiropractic, this is [name]. How may I help you?"

Schedule a new practice member

  1. Get the caller's name.
  2. Get the best callback number in case the call disconnects.
  3. Use a two-choice close: ask whether morning or afternoon is better, then offer approved times.
  4. Ask whom to thank for the referral.
  5. Confirm date/time and explain that a text will contain appointment details, address, and the online paperwork link.
  6. Enter the lead/appointment and required notes before moving to the next task.
NPM CONFIRMATION

"Hello [name], this is [name] calling from Revive Chiropractic to confirm your appointment for [day/date] at [time]. Great - we look forward to seeing you then."

ROF CONFIRMATION

"Hello [name], this is [name] calling from Revive Chiropractic to confirm your appointment for [day/date] at [time]. This is an important appointment, and we look forward to seeing you then."

Follow-up standard

Identify yourself and Revive, state the approved reason for the call, connect it to the person's prior inquiry/referral/event, and offer a morning-or-afternoon choice. Use only current promotions. For voicemail, leave the approved callback number and no private health details. Document the outcome immediately.

Lead follow-up scripts

Online voucher / social lead

“Hello [name], this is [your name] calling from Revive Chiropractic. I saw that you claimed one of our current new-patient offers, and I wanted to help schedule your first appointment. Would a morning or afternoon appointment work better?”

Screening or event lead

Thank the person for visiting the booth, reference only the concern they supplied, and offer two approved appointment choices. If they paid a deposit, note it in the account; if the consultation was complimentary, use the current approved offer.

Lunch & Learn or referral

Thank them for speaking with the team or identify the referring practice member, then offer the current approved consultation and a morning-or-afternoon choice. Do not promise services, X-rays, or pricing beyond the active offer.

Voicemail: Identify yourself and Revive, state that you are calling about their appointment request, leave the approved callback number, and omit health details.

Missed ROF / Doctor’s Report

“Hello [name], this is [your name] calling from Revive Chiropractic. We had you scheduled to review your X-rays with the doctor and would like to reschedule that appointment. Would [approved option one] or [approved option two] work better?”

Schedule the new appointment before ending the call when possible. Update the appointment code and add a dated note with initials.

Common questions

Insurance: “We can verify your chiropractic benefits before quoting your expected responsibility. Verification is not a guarantee of payment.”

Cost or visits: Every case is different. The doctor will evaluate the patient and explain the recommended care.

First visit length: Allow approximately 45–60 minutes.

Bring prior X-rays/MRI? Yes, ask the patient to bring available reports/images for the doctor to review.

Same-day adjustment or X-rays? The doctor determines what is appropriate after the evaluation.

Special situations - stop and route

  • Patient information: never disclose it to an unverified caller. Follow the current identity-verification and authorization process.
  • Stopping care: take a neutral message and advise that the office manager will return the call.
  • Payment-plan change: do not change terms by phone; arrange the manager-approved review process.
  • Illness: do not provide medical advice. Take the message and route clinical questions to the doctor; follow current infection-control direction.
  • Doctor/manager request: advise that they are with a patient or unavailable, ask whether you can help, and take a complete message.
  • Media inquiry: make no statement; direct it to the doctor/management.

FAQ boundaries

For cost, number of visits, X-rays, timing of an adjustment, or clinical suitability, explain that every case differs and the doctor must evaluate the person. Offer the current consultation/first-visit option without guaranteeing insurance payment, results, visit count, or treatment.
