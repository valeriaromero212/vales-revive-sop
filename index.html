SHIFT CONTROL

Daily Procedures

9:00 a.m. opening checklist

  1. Start computers and log into Platinum; start the server first.
  2. Set adjustment and consultation rooms to the EHR screen; set the check-in computer to Arrivals.
  3. Run the X-ray computer test blank and turn on the SoftWave unit.
  4. Review and respond to Sked messages and voicemails.
  5. Enter new leads from HighLevel and Sked, then check the office inbox.
  6. Call any same-day new practice members or Reports of Findings that are still unconfirmed. Then confirm the next business day's new practice members and Reports of Findings.
  7. Check submitted intakes and upload them to the correct patient account.
  8. Verify pending insurance items, prioritizing upcoming appointments.
  9. Complete the room-readiness check below.
  10. Turn on the diffuser. Open curtains and unlock the front door at 10:00 a.m.

Room-readiness check

  • No trash in rooms
  • Tables cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed
  • Counters wiped
  • Bathroom clean and stocked
  • Face paper and paper towels stocked
  • Kids' area picked up
  • Spray bottles full; cloths replaced
  • SoftWave room stocked with gowns and cloths
  • Cups, napkins, tea, and water available

2:45 p.m. reset

  1. Reset the diffuser.
  2. Review texts and voicemails received during the break.
  3. Repeat the light-cleaning and room-readiness check.
  4. Open curtains and unlock the door at 3:00 p.m.

End-of-day checklist

  1. Confirm all next-day new practice member and Report of Findings appointments.
  2. Scan and upload all documents to the correct accounts.
  3. Schedule first-adjustment texts in Sked that need to send.
  4. Restock forms for the next day and finish next-day insurance verification.
  5. Take out trash; clean and reset rooms.
  6. Turn off SoftWave.
  7. Shut down computers and confirm the Platinum backup completes.
  8. If last out: check every room, lock the front glass and back office doors, and turn off lights and music.

Daily appointment and message review

  1. Open today’s Platinum calendar.
  2. Call any unconfirmed new practice member or Report of Findings appointments. Confirm them before check-in.
  3. Confirm the next business day’s new practice member and Report of Findings appointments.
  4. Check Sked, voicemail, and email. Return messages and note each call with the date and your initials.
  5. Check that every first-adjustment message is scheduled for the right patient and time.

When you have 10 minutes

Use extra time for follow-up calls, looking for community events, scheduling health talks, or light cleaning. Straighten the chairs and toys, wipe counters and tables, clean windows and mirrors, replace table paper, and pick up anything on the floor. Shred only when approved and when no patients are in the office.

PATIENT JOURNEY

New Practice Member - Day 1

Day 1 procedure

  1. Check the online intake and Platinum profile before the patient arrives. Make sure the name, address, phone, date of birth, referral, insurance, and F5 notes are complete.
  2. Welcome the patient by name and introduce yourself. If forms are unfinished, give them the intake paperwork. Copy the driver’s license and insurance card, then return both.
  3. If the appointment is U or 2, confirm it and change the code before check-in. Update the patient information, then mark them arrived.
  4. Fill out the Consultation History/Day 1 and Exam Form/Range of Motion in red pen. Put them with the intake on the clipboard. Offer tea or water.
  5. Give the clipboard to the patient coordinator and walk the patient to an open consultation room.
  6. The visit goes in this order: consultation, doctor’s exam, X-rays, then SoftWave mapping when it is listed for the visit.
  7. Schedule the ROF, collect the Day 1 fee, give the receipt, and explain that confirmation and reminder texts will be sent.
  8. Scan the intake, ID, insurance verification, Day 1 form, exam form, signed receipt unless cash, and any Optum forms or MRI reports into the patient’s account.
WELCOME

"Welcome to Revive Chiropractic. My name is Valeria. While you finish the paperwork, I'll copy your driver's license and insurance card so we can verify it for you. Please let me know if you have any questions."

X-ray room

Pull up the correct patient on the X-ray computer. Ask the patient to remove all metal items and empty their pockets. Let the doctor know when the patient is ready.

03

PATIENT JOURNEY

Report of Findings (ROF)

ROF steps

  1. Verify insurance and enter the benefits. Have the Day 1 papers and care plan ready.
  2. Check the patient in, offer tea or water, walk them to the consultation room, and give the clipboard to the doctor.
  3. Process the option they chose: pay in full, monthly, or CareCredit.
  4. Create the calendar, add the appointment codes, issue the member card, and show them how to swipe in.
  5. Show them the adjustment rooms, where personal items go, how to check in on the room computer, and explain that the table noises are normal.
  6. After the adjustment, send them to SoftWave or the front based on the care plan. Remind them of the next visit.
  7. Upload the signed care plan, receipt, recurring agreement, and CareCredit papers if used. Enter the payment, plan notes, dates, cost, visits, services, codes, stop managers, and first-adjustment message in Sked.

First-adjustment message timing

  1. Open Sked after the patient completes the first adjustment.
  2. If the adjustment was in the morning, schedule the message for that afternoon.
  3. If the adjustment was in the afternoon, schedule the message for the next morning.
  4. Before saving, check the patient name, phone number, template, send date, and send time.

Payments & Pricing

Taking a payment

  1. Open the patient account and read the F5 notes.
  2. Match the amount and payment option to the signed care plan or the service listed for the visit.
  3. Confirm whether it is pay in full, monthly, CareCredit, or pay per visit.
  4. Take the payment in Fortis and save the card to the Fortis vault.
  5. Give the patient the receipt and upload the signed copy when required.
  6. Enter the payment and any needed note in Platinum before closing the account.

Price reference

  • $59 new-patient special: consultation and X-rays
  • $29 referral new-patient special
  • $67 decompression
  • $47 for each therapy added to a new-patient special

Check the patient account and selected service in Platinum before charging.

Payment questions

  • Never discuss a balance, payment, or care plan where other patients can hear.
  • If a patient wants to change a payment plan, schedule a private time with the office manager.
  • For pay-per-visit patients, charge the rate listed in the F5 notes at every visit.

PLATINUM WORKFLOW

Platinum Step-by-Step

Create the calendar after ROF

  1. Right-click in the appointment box and select Add.
  2. For a 36-visit plan, select Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday in the 3X column and enter the number of weeks listed on the patient’s signed plan.
  3. Enter the patient’s preferred time for each selected day. Review appointment history when trying to preserve their usual day and time.
  4. Exit to save, then open the Patient Calendar (pink box).
  5. Enter 1 at visit 2, E at visit 6, R at visits 12 and 24, X at visit 36, and P at visit 37.
  6. Print the calendar with 1 → C.

Set up the care plan

  1. Open the patient account and select Plan Start.
  2. Enter the actual care start date.
  3. Enter the patient portion from the signed care plan—not from memory.
  4. Enter the allowed number of visits.
  5. Select End Care Plan by Visits and Care Plan Option.
  6. If applicable, select Keep Billing Insurance.
  7. Exit to save; confirm the care-plan box turns blue.

Set up services

  1. Open the Service 1–6 link under the care plan.
  2. Set Service #1 to the adjustment service shown on the signed plan. For insurance, use INS-ADJ 3–4 and enter the verified copay.
  3. Use Service #3 for therapeutic exercises for ASH patients only.
  4. When insurance coverage or active care ends, switch the service to Wellness.

Add stop managers

  1. From F5 notes, open Stop Manager; use F4 to add entries.
  2. Add: 3 Block (if applicable), 7 Exercises, 12 remind first reassessment, 13 first reassessment, 23 remind second reassessment, 24 second reassessment, 35 remind re-X-ray, and 36 re-X-ray.
  3. Add a service-change reminder where insurance or active care ends.
  4. Add date-based reminders for payments, updated UHC forms, or other account needs.
  5. Review every entry against the signed plan before saving.

QUICK REFERENCE

What Each Treatment Does

Chiropractic adjustment

An adjustment is used to correct misalignments in the spine. This helps take pressure off the nervous system and allows the body to function and heal better.

NMR / wobble chair

Neuromuscular re-education uses movement on the wobble chair to strengthen the lower back, hydrate the discs, help the adjustment hold, and stabilize the hips.

The patient sits on the stability disc and moves the hips in the directions they were shown.

Cervical traction

Cervical traction stretches the neck to reduce stiffness, improve flexibility, and help restore the curve in the neck.

The setup is different for each patient. Follow the position and timing listed for that patient.

Graston

Graston is a soft-tissue therapy that uses a metal tool and lotion to work on muscle adhesions and scar tissue. It is used to improve movement in the area and decrease pain.

SoftWave

SoftWave is a tissue-regeneration therapy that sends sound waves into the treatment area. It is used to stimulate the body's healing response, decrease inflammation and pain, and improve range of motion.

Decompression

Decompression gently stretches the spine to reduce pressure on the discs and nerves. The level and length of the session are set for each patient.

SoftWave aftercare

After the first SoftWave treatment, give the patient the SoftWave aftercare sheet and review these three points for the next 2–3 days:

  1. No ice, Advil, or ibuprofen.
  2. Rest. Avoid rigorous exercise so the treated area is not put under extra stress.
  3. Hydrate. Encourage the patient to drink plenty of water.

Let the patient know that some soreness can be normal after treatment.

Verification workflow

  1. Have the verification form, member ID, date of birth, provider information, and insurance portal or phone number ready.
  2. Write down the effective and renewal dates, chiropractic benefits, network status, copay or coinsurance, deductible and amount met, out-of-pocket maximum and amount met, visit limit and visits remaining, and authorization requirements.

QUICK ANSWERS

FAQ

Payments and care plans

Can I discuss the patient’s payment or care plan at the front desk?

No. Never discuss payments, balances, or care-plan details where other patients can hear. Move the conversation somewhere private.

What do I do after taking a card payment in Fortis?

Save the payment method to the Fortis vault so it is on file for future payments.

What should I check before charging?

Match the payment option, amount, service code, and recurring date to the signed care plan.

What if a patient wants to change a payment plan?

Schedule a time for the patient to go over it with the office manager.

New patient questions

Can I be adjusted on the first day?

Do not say yes or no. Say: “That will be based on the doctor’s recommendations after your evaluation.”

Will I need X-rays?

“Every case is different. The doctor will decide what is needed after your evaluation.”

How much will it cost or how many visits will I need?

“Every case is different. The doctor will evaluate you and go over any recommended care with you.”

How long is the first visit?

Allow about 45–60 minutes.

Should I bring previous X-rays or an MRI?

Yes. Ask the patient to bring any images or reports they have for the doctor to review.

Insurance and scheduling

Do you take my insurance?

“We can verify your chiropractic benefits for you. The insurance company makes the final payment decision.”

Can a walk-in be seen?

Get their ID and insurance card, confirm the address and contact information, then check for an available time before promising an appointment.

Can I check in an unconfirmed new patient or ROF?

No. Confirm the appointment first, change U or 2 to the correct code, then check the patient in.

How should I schedule future appointments?

Check the patient’s appointment history and keep their usual days and times when possible.

Are there missed-appointment fees?

No missed-appointment fee is charged.

Special phone calls and privacy

Can I give patient information over the phone?

Verify who is calling before giving any information. Never discuss patient information with someone who is not authorized.

What if someone calls to stop care?

Take a message and let them know the office manager will call them back.

What if a patient says they are sick?

Take the message and send any clinical questions to the doctor.

What if someone asks for the doctor or office manager?

Let them know the doctor or manager is with a patient or unavailable. Ask if you can help, then take a complete message.

What if the media calls?

Take their name, number, organization, and reason for calling. Send the message to the doctor or office manager.

Treatments and patient comfort

What if the patient feels pain or dizziness during an exercise or therapy?

Stop right away and get the doctor.

What should I say about treatment recommendations?

Do not diagnose or recommend treatment. Let the patient know the doctor will go over what is appropriate for them.

What if a patient has questions after the Report of Findings?

Write down the question and send it to the office manager or doctor. Do not change the plan, price, or recommendation yourself.
