01

SHIFT CONTROL

Daily Procedures

9:00 a.m. opening checklist

  1. Start computers and log into Platinum; start the server first.
  2. Set adjustment and consultation rooms to the EHR screen; set the check-in computer to Arrivals.
  3. Run the X-ray computer test blank and turn on the SoftWave unit.
  4. Review and respond to Sked messages and voicemails.
  5. Enter new leads from approved lead sources and check the office inbox.
  6. Call any same-day new practice members or Reports of Findings that are still unconfirmed. Then confirm the next business day's new practice members and Reports of Findings.
  7. Check submitted intakes and upload them to the correct patient account.
  8. Verify pending insurance items, prioritizing upcoming appointments.
  9. Complete the room-readiness check below.
  10. Turn on the diffuser. Open curtains and unlock the front door at 10:00 a.m.

Room-readiness check

  • No trash in rooms
  • Tables cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed
  • Counters wiped
  • Bathroom clean and stocked
  • Face paper and paper towels stocked
  • Kids' area picked up
  • Spray bottles full; cloths replaced
  • SoftWave room stocked with gowns and cloths
  • Cups, napkins, tea, and water available

2:45 p.m. reset

  1. Reset the diffuser.
  2. Review texts and voicemails received during the break.
  3. Repeat the light-cleaning and room-readiness check.
  4. Open curtains and unlock the door at 3:00 p.m.

End-of-day checklist

  1. Confirm all next-day new practice member and Report of Findings appointments.
  2. Scan and upload all documents to the correct accounts.
  3. Schedule first-adjustment texts in Sked that need to send.
  4. Restock forms for the next day and finish next-day insurance verification.
  5. Take out trash; clean and reset rooms.
  6. Turn off SoftWave.
  7. Shut down computers and confirm the Platinum backup completes.
  8. If last out: check every room, lock the front glass and back office doors, and turn off lights and music.

Daily appointment and message review

  1. Open the Platinum calendar and review today first.
  2. Check every new practice member and Report of Findings appointment. If it is still unconfirmed, call before checking the patient in.
  3. Review the next business day and confirm all new practice member and Report of Findings appointments.
  4. Open Sked, voicemail, and the office inbox. Return messages and document each call with the date and your initials.
  5. Before leaving, make sure all first-adjustment messages are scheduled for the correct patient, date, and time.

When you have 10 minutes

Use extra time for follow-up calls, looking for community events, scheduling health talks, or light cleaning. Straighten the chairs and toys, wipe counters and tables, clean windows and mirrors, replace table paper, and pick up anything on the floor. Shred only when approved and when no patients are in the office.

02

PATIENT JOURNEY

New Practice Member - Day 1

Day 1 procedure

  1. Prepare before arrival. Check online intake status and create/confirm the Platinum profile. Review name, address, phone, date of birth, referral source, insurance, and important F5 notes.
  2. Welcome them by name. Introduce yourself. If the forms are not finished, give them the intake paperwork. Ask for their driver's license and insurance card, make the copies needed for verification, and give the originals right back.
  3. Confirm, update, then check in. Do not check the patient in while the appointment is coded U or 2. Confirm the appointment, change it to the correct confirmed code, update their information and insurance, and then mark them arrived.
  4. Build the visit packet. Complete the two current red-pen forms: Consultation History/Day 1 and Exam Form/Range of Motion. Place them with the intake on the designated clipboard. Offer tea or water.
  5. Walk them back. Give the clipboard to the patient coordinator and walk the patient to an open consultation room.
  6. Day 1 flow. Consultation, doctor's exam, X-rays, and SoftWave mapping when needed.
  7. Finish Day 1. Schedule the ROF, collect the Day 1 fee, give the patient a receipt, and let them know they will receive confirmation and reminder texts.
  8. Upload everything. Scan the intake, ID and insurance verification, Day 1 form, completed exam form, signed receipt when needed, and any other records into the patient's account.
WELCOME

"Welcome to Revive Chiropractic. My name is [name]. While you finish the paperwork, I'll copy your driver's license and insurance card so we can verify it for you. Please let me know if you have any questions."

X-ray room

Pull up the correct patient on the X-ray computer. Ask the patient to remove all metal items and empty their pockets. Let the doctor know when the patient is ready.

Remember: Do not discuss care-plan finances where other patients can hear. If the patient asks about a same-day adjustment, let them know the doctor will decide after the evaluation.
03

PATIENT JOURNEY

Report of Findings (ROF)

ROF steps

  1. Before they arrive. Make sure insurance is verified and the benefit information is entered. Have the Day 1 papers and care plan ready.
  2. Check them in. Offer tea or water, walk them to the consultation room, and give the clipboard to the doctor.
  3. Process the plan they chose. Follow the steps for pay in full, monthly payments, or CareCredit.
  4. Schedule care. Create the calendar, apply the correct appointment codes, issue the member card, and review how to swipe in.
  5. Show them the adjustment room. Explain the room numbers, where to put personal items, how to check in on the computer, and that the table noises are normal.
  6. Close the visit. After the adjustment, direct the patient to SoftWave or the front as the care plan requires and remind them of the next visit.
  7. Complete the account. Upload the signed care plan, receipt, recurring agreement, and CareCredit records if used; enter payment, plan notes, plan dates/cost/visits, services, coded visits, stop managers, and the first-adjustment message in Sked.
Before charging: Match the payment option, amount, service code, and recurring date to the signed care plan.

First-adjustment message timing

  1. Open Sked after the patient completes the first adjustment.
  2. If the adjustment was in the morning, schedule the message for that afternoon.
  3. If the adjustment was in the afternoon, schedule the message for the next morning.
  4. Before saving, check the patient name, phone number, template, send date, and send time.
04

PLATINUM WORKFLOW

Scheduling & Care Plans

Create the calendar after ROF

  1. Right-click in the appointment box and select Add.
  2. For a 36-visit plan, select Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday in the 3X column and enter the number of weeks listed on the patient’s signed plan.
  3. Enter the patient’s preferred time for each selected day. Review appointment history when trying to preserve their usual day and time.
  4. Exit to save, then open the Patient Calendar (pink box).
  5. Enter 1 at visit 2, E at visit 6, R at visits 12 and 24, X at visit 36, and P at visit 37.
  6. Print the calendar with 1 → C.

Set up the care plan

  1. Open the patient account and select Plan Start.
  2. Enter the actual care start date.
  3. Enter the patient portion from the signed care plan—not from memory.
  4. Enter the allowed number of visits.
  5. Select End Care Plan by Visits and Care Plan Option.
  6. If applicable, select Keep Billing Insurance.
  7. Exit to save; confirm the care-plan box turns blue.

Set up services

  1. Open the Service 1–6 link under the care plan.
  2. Set Service #1 to the correct adjustment service. For insurance, use INS-ADJ 3–4 and enter the verified copay.
  3. Use Service #3 for therapeutic exercises for ASH patients only.
  4. When insurance coverage or active care ends, switch the service to Wellness.

Add stop managers

  1. From F5 notes, open Stop Manager; use F4 to add entries.
  2. Add: 3 Block (if applicable), 7 Exercises, 12 remind first reassessment, 13 first reassessment, 23 remind second reassessment, 24 second reassessment, 35 remind re-X-ray, and 36 re-X-ray.
  3. Add a service-change reminder where insurance or active care ends.
  4. Add date-based reminders for payments, updated UHC forms, or other account needs.
  5. Review every entry against the signed plan before saving.

ASSIGNED CARE

Services & Therapies

NMR / wobble chair

  1. Check the appointment code to make sure the patient is starting exercises.

  3. When they finish, send them to the next clean adjusting room.

Cervical traction

SoftWave

  3. Document completion and tell the doctor about any discomfort or equipment concerns.

Other therapies

For Graston or another therapy, check what is listed for the appointment, prepare the room and supplies, keep the patient moving through the visit, and enter the correct service when completed.

QUICK REFERENCE

What Each Treatment Does

Chiropractic adjustment

An adjustment is used to correct misalignments in the spine. This helps take pressure off the nervous system and allows the body to function and heal better.

NMR / wobble chair

Neuromuscular re-education uses movement on the wobble chair to strengthen the lower back, hydrate the discs, help the adjustment hold, and stabilize the hips.

The patient sits on the stability disc and moves the hips in the directions they were shown.

Cervical traction

Cervical traction stretches the neck to reduce stiffness, improve flexibility, and help restore the curve in the neck.

The setup is different for each patient. Follow the position and timing listed for that patient.

Graston

Graston is a soft-tissue therapy that uses a metal tool and lotion to work on muscle adhesions and scar tissue. It is used to improve movement in the area and decrease pain.

SoftWave

SoftWave is a tissue-regeneration therapy that sends sound waves into the treatment area. It is used to stimulate the body's healing response, decrease inflammation and pain, and improve range of motion.

Decompression

Decompression gently stretches the spine to reduce pressure on the discs and nerves. The level and length of the session are set for each patient.

SoftWave aftercare

After the first SoftWave treatment, give the patient the SoftWave aftercare sheet and review these three points for the next 2–3 days:

  1. No ice, Advil, or ibuprofen.
  2. Rest. Avoid rigorous exercise so the treated area is not put under extra stress.
  3. Hydrate. Encourage the patient to drink plenty of water.

Let the patient know that some soreness can be normal after treatment.

PATIENT COVERAGE

Insurance Verification

Verification workflow

  1. Get everything ready. Have the verification form, member ID, date of birth, insurance portal or phone number, and provider information in front of you.
  2. Check eligibility and benefits. Write down the effective date, renewal date, chiropractic benefits, network status, copay or coinsurance, deductible and amount met, out-of-pocket maximum and amount met, visit limit and visits remaining, and whether authorization is needed.
  3. Use the correct insurance portal or number. Use ASH, Availity, Optum/UHC, Medicare, or the number on the back of the insurance card as listed below.
  4. Document the verification. Add the representative's name, call reference number, your name, the date, the portal result, and anything that is still pending.
  5. Finish the account. Upload the verification and enter the benefit information in Platinum. If something is unclear, ask before quoting the patient's amount.

COMMUNICATION

Phone & Follow-up

STANDARD GREETING

"Thank you for calling Revive Chiropractic, this is [name]. How may I help you?"

Schedule a new practice member

  1. Ask for the caller's name.
  2. Ask for the best callback number in case the call disconnects.
  3. Ask whether morning or afternoon is better, then give them two available times.
  4. Ask who referred them.
  5. Repeat the appointment day and time. Let them know the confirmation text includes the office address and online paperwork.
  6. Enter the new patient in Platinum, schedule the appointment, and add any notes from the call.
NPM CONFIRMATION

"Hello [name], this is [name] calling from Revive Chiropractic to confirm your appointment for [day/date] at [time]. Great - we look forward to seeing you then."

ROF CONFIRMATION

"Hello [name], this is [name] calling from Revive Chiropractic to confirm your appointment for [day/date] at [time]. This is an important appointment, and we look forward to seeing you then."

How to make a follow-up call

Introduce yourself and say you are calling from Revive. Explain why you are calling and mention the person's inquiry, referral, or event. Ask whether morning or afternoon works better, then offer an available time. If you leave a voicemail, give the office callback number. Add the result of the call, the date, and your initials to the account.

Lead follow-up scripts

Online voucher / social lead

“Hello [name], this is [your name] calling from Revive Chiropractic. I saw that you claimed one of our current new-patient offers, and I wanted to help schedule your first appointment. Would a morning or afternoon appointment work better?”

Screening or event lead

Thank the person for visiting the booth, reference only the concern they supplied, and offer two approved appointment choices. If they paid a deposit, note it in the account; if the consultation was complimentary, use the current approved offer.

Lunch & Learn or referral

Thank them for speaking with the team or identify the referring practice member, then offer the current approved consultation and a morning-or-afternoon choice. Do not promise services, X-rays, or pricing beyond the active offer.

Voicemail: Identify yourself and Revive, state that you are calling about their appointment request, leave the approved callback number, and omit health details.

Missed ROF / Doctor’s Report

“Hello [name], this is [your name] calling from Revive Chiropractic. We had you scheduled to review your X-rays with the doctor and would like to reschedule that appointment. Would [approved option one] or [approved option two] work better?”

Schedule the new appointment before ending the call when possible. Update the appointment code and add a dated note with initials.

Common questions

Insurance: “We can verify your chiropractic benefits before quoting your expected responsibility. Verification is not a guarantee of payment.”

Cost or visits: Every case is different. The doctor will evaluate the patient and explain the recommended care.

First visit length: Allow approximately 45–60 minutes.

Bring prior X-rays/MRI? Yes, ask the patient to bring available reports/images for the doctor to review.

Same-day adjustment or X-rays? The doctor determines what is appropriate after the evaluation.

Special phone calls

  • Patient information: Verify who is calling before giving any information.
  • Stopping care: Take a message and let them know the office manager will call them back.
  • Changing a payment plan: Schedule a time for the patient to review it with the office manager.
  • Patient is sick: Take the message and send any clinical questions to the doctor.
  • Asking for the doctor or manager: Let the caller know they are with a patient or unavailable, ask if you can help, and take a complete message.
  • Media call: Take their contact information and send it to the doctor or office manager.

Questions for the doctor

If someone asks about cost, number of visits, X-rays, when they will be adjusted, or what treatment they need, explain that every case is different and the doctor will go over it after the evaluation.
