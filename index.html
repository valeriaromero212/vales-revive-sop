SHIFT CONTROL

Daily Procedures

9:00 a.m. opening checklist

  1. Start computers and log into Platinum; start the server first.
  2. Set adjustment and consultation rooms to the EHR screen; set the check-in computer to Arrivals.
  3. Run the X-ray computer test blank and turn on the SoftWave unit.
  4. Review and respond to Sked messages and voicemails.
  5. Enter new leads from HighLevel and Sked, then check the office inbox.
  6. Call any same-day new practice members or Reports of Findings that are still unconfirmed. Then confirm the next business day's new practice members and Reports of Findings.
  7. Check submitted intakes and upload them to the correct patient account.
  8. Verify pending insurance items, prioritizing upcoming appointments.
  9. Complete the room-readiness check below.
  10. Turn on the diffuser. Open curtains and unlock the front door at 10:00 a.m.

Room-readiness check

  • No trash in rooms
  • Tables cleaned
  • Floors vacuumed
  • Counters wiped
  • Bathroom clean and stocked
  • Face paper and paper towels stocked
  • Kids' area picked up
  • Spray bottles full; cloths replaced
  • SoftWave room stocked with gowns and cloths
  • Cups, napkins, tea, and water available

2:45 p.m. reset

  1. Reset the diffuser.
  2. Review texts and voicemails received during the break.
  3. Repeat the light-cleaning and room-readiness check.
  4. Open curtains and unlock the door at 3:00 p.m.

End-of-day checklist

  1. Confirm all next-day new practice member and Report of Findings appointments.
  2. Scan and upload all documents to the correct accounts.
  3. Schedule first-adjustment texts in Sked that need to send.
  4. Restock forms for the next day and finish next-day insurance verification.
  5. Take out trash; clean and reset rooms.
  6. Turn off SoftWave.
  7. Shut down computers and confirm the Platinum backup completes.
  8. If last out: check every room, lock the front glass and back office doors, and turn off lights and music.

Daily appointment and message review

  1. Open today’s Platinum calendar.
  2. Call any unconfirmed new practice member or Report of Findings appointments. Confirm them before check-in.
  3. Call Tuesday appointments on Monday, Thursday appointments on Tuesday, and Monday appointments on Thursday.
  4. Confirm all next-day new practice member and Report of Findings appointments.
  5. Check Sked, voicemail, and email. Return messages and note each call with the date and your initials.
  6. Check that every first-adjustment message is scheduled for the right patient and time.

When you have 10 minutes

Use extra time for follow-up calls, looking for community events, scheduling health talks, or light cleaning. Straighten the chairs and toys, wipe counters and tables, clean windows and mirrors, replace table paper, and pick up anything on the floor. When approved, place documents in the shred bin. The shredding company will pick them up.

Paper files

  1. Keep papers the patient still needs to sign at the front of the paper file.
  2. This includes receipts, care plans, and any other signature pages.
  3. After the papers are signed, scan and upload them to the patient account.
  4. File the originals or place them in the shred bin when approved.

PATIENT JOURNEY

New Practice Member - Day 1

Day 1 procedure

  1. Send the intake link after scheduling. Check for the completed intake before arrival.
  2. Start the Platinum profile with the name, address, phone, date of birth, referral, insurance, and F5 notes from the intake.
  3. Prefill the Consultation History/Day 1 and Exam Form/Range of Motion from the intake.
  4. Welcome the patient by name. Give them the intake paperwork when it was not completed online.
  5. Copy the driver’s license and insurance card. Confirm that the address on the license is current, then return both cards.
  6. If the appointment is U or 2, confirm it and change the code before check-in. Update the patient information, then mark them arrived.
  7. Finish the Day 1 and Exam forms in red pen. Put them with the intake on the clipboard. Offer tea or water.
  8. Give the clipboard to the patient coordinator and walk the patient to an open consultation room.
  9. The visit goes in this order: consultation, doctor’s exam, X-rays, then SoftWave mapping when it is listed for the visit.
  10. Complete the insurance verification and let the patient know the benefits will be reviewed at the next visit.
  11. Collect the Day 1 fee after X-rays: $59 new-patient special, $29 referral special, or the amount listed for the visit.
  12. Schedule the ROF, give the receipt, and explain that confirmation and reminder texts will be sent.
  13. Scan the intake, ID, insurance verification, Day 1 form, exam form, signed receipt unless cash, and any Optum forms or MRI reports into the patient’s account.

New-patient walk-in

  1. Create the appointment and send the intake link.
  2. Check the current block for a new-patient or ROF appointment before adding another long appointment.
  3. Give the patient the intake paperwork when they are completing it in the office.
  4. Continue with the Day 1 procedure above.

PATIENT JOURNEY

Report of Findings (ROF)

ROF steps

  1. Verify insurance and enter the benefits. Have the Day 1 papers and care plan ready.
  2. Check the patient in, offer tea or water, walk them to the consultation room, and give the clipboard to the doctor.
  3. Use the care plan prepared by Dianna. Review the recommended visits and the payment options listed on the plan.
  4. Process the selected option: pay in full, installments, insurance, CareCredit, or pay per visit.
  5. Create the calendar, add the appointment codes, issue the member card, and show them how to swipe in.
  6. Show them the adjustment rooms, where personal items go, how to check in on the room computer, and explain that the table noises are normal.
  7. After the adjustment, send them to SoftWave or the front based on the care plan. Remind them of the next visit.
  8. Upload the signed care plan, receipt, recurring agreement, and CareCredit papers if used. Enter the payment, plan notes, dates, cost, visits, services, codes, stop managers, and first-adjustment message in Sked.

First-adjustment message timing

  1. Open Sked after the patient completes the first adjustment.
  2. If the adjustment was in the morning, schedule the message for that afternoon.
  3. If the adjustment was in the afternoon, schedule the message for the next morning.
  4. Before saving, check the patient name, phone number, template, send date, and send time.

  8. Give the patient the receipt and upload the signed copy when required.
  9. Add the payment note before closing the account.

  1. Open the Service 1–6 link under the care plan.

  2. Add: 3 Block (if applicable), 7 Exercises, 12 remind first reassessment, 13 first reassessment, 23 remind second reassessment, 24 second reassessment, 35 remind re-X-ray, and 36 re-X-ray.
  3. Add a service-change reminder where insurance or active care ends.

  5. Review every entry against the signed plan before saving.

QUICK REFERENCE

What Each Treatment Does

Chiropractic adjustment

An adjustment is used to correct misalignments in the spine. This helps take pressure off the nervous system and allows the body to function and heal better.

NMR / wobble chair

Neuromuscular re-education uses movement on the wobble chair to strengthen the lower back, hydrate the discs, help the adjustment hold, and stabilize the hips.

The patient sits on the stability disc and moves the hips in the directions they were shown.

Cervical traction

Cervical traction stretches the neck to reduce stiffness, improve flexibility, and help restore the curve in the neck.

The setup is different for each patient. Follow the position and timing listed for that patient.

Graston

Graston is a soft-tissue therapy that uses a metal tool and lotion to work on muscle adhesions and scar tissue. It is used to improve movement in the area and decrease pain.

SoftWave

SoftWave is a tissue-regeneration therapy that sends sound waves into the treatment area. It is used to stimulate the body's healing response, decrease inflammation and pain, and improve range of motion.

Decompression

Decompression gently stretches the spine to reduce pressure on the discs and nerves. The level and length of the session are set for each patient.

SoftWave aftercare

After the first SoftWave treatment, give the patient the SoftWave aftercare sheet and review these three points for the next 2–3 days:

  1. No ice, Advil, or ibuprofen.
  2. Rest. Avoid rigorous exercise so the treated area is not put under extra stress.
  3. Hydrate. Encourage the patient to drink plenty of water.

Let the patient know that some soreness can be normal after treatment.

Important Notes

Payments and care plans
  • Private conversations: Move payment, balance, and care-plan conversations away from other patients.
  • Before charging: Match the amount, payment option, service code, and recurring date to the signed care plan.
  • Fortis: Save the payment method to the vault for future payments.
  • Payment-plan changes: Schedule an in-office conversation so the plan can be reviewed with the patient.
New-patient answers
  • When will I get my first adjustment?
    “Every person and case is different, and depending on the care you will need and what diagnostic tests may be necessary, will determine that. But we do offer you a complimentary consultation, so the doctor can evaluate your case and help to determine that.”
  • Will I need X-rays?
    “Every person and case is different, and depending on the care you will need and what diagnostic tests may be necessary, will determine that. But we do offer you a complimentary consultation, so the doctor can evaluate your case and help to determine that.”
  • How much will it cost?
    “Every case is different. But we do offer you a complimentary insurance benefits check to see what your insurance covers and a complimentary consultation, so the doctor can determine the specifics of your case and estimate the costs before you incur any charges.”
  • How many visits will I need?
    “Every case is different, it is difficult to be sure how many visits you will need before the doctor has had a chance to evaluate you and determine what care, if any, you will need. But we do offer you a complimentary consultation, so the doctor can determine the specifics of your case and estimate the costs before you incur any charges.”
  • How long will it take?
    “You can expect your first visit and evaluation to take approximately 45 to 60 minutes.”
  • I have X-rays or an MRI. Should I bring them with me?
    “Yes.”
Insurance and scheduling
  • Insurance answer: “Yes, we accept all major insurances. We offer a complimentary benefits check when you come in, so you’ll know exactly what your insurance covers before you incur any charges.”
  • Office hours: “We are open Monday, Tuesday and Thursday from 10am–1pm, and 3pm–7pm.”
  • New-patient walk-ins: Collect the ID and insurance card, confirm the address and contact information, then check for an available time.
  • Unconfirmed new patient or ROF: Confirm the appointment, change U or 2 to the correct code, then check the patient in.
  • Future appointments: Check the appointment history and keep the patient’s usual days and times when possible.
  • Missed appointments: There is no missed-appointment fee.
Phone calls and privacy
  • Patient information: Never give patient information over the phone.
  • Stopping care: Take a message and let the patient know the office manager will call them to take care of everything.
  • Sick patient: Follow the office script. If the patient is extremely sick, let them stay home.
  • Doctor or Dianna requested: Let the caller know they are with a patient. Ask if you can help or take a message.
  • Media call: Take the caller’s name, number, organization, and reason for calling. Send the message to the doctor or office manager.
Treatment and patient comfort
  • Pain or dizziness: Stop the exercise or therapy and get the doctor.
  • Treatment recommendation: “The doctor will go over the appropriate treatment with you.”
  • Same-day adjustment: Let the patient know it depends on the doctor’s recommendation. Do not promise or rule it out.
  • Questions after the ROF: Write down the question and send it to the office manager or doctor.
